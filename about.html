🌾 About AI Smart Farming Assistant

AI Smart Farming Assistant is an intelligent agriculture support system designed to help farmers make better farming decisions using Artificial Intelligence and modern technology.

Our Features

🌿
Disease Detection

Upload a plant leaf image to identify possible diseases and get treatment and prevention guidance.

🤖
AI Agriculture Chatbot

Ask agriculture-related questions in English or Marathi and receive AI-based guidance.

🌦️
Weather Information

Get current and upcoming weather information useful for farming decisions.

💧
Irrigation Advice

Get irrigation suggestions based on crop, growth stage, soil and weather conditions.

🌱
Fertilizer Recommendation

Receive AI-based fertilizer recommendations according to crop and growth stage.

💰
Market Prices

Check the latest available mandi market prices for agricultural commodities.
